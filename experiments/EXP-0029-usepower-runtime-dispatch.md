## Evidence interpretation

- **Confirmed:** the helper performs the same `+0x184`, then `+0x134` virtual-slot sequence on one validated object pointer; the `UsePower` and parent vtables have distinct exact targets in those slots.
- **Strong candidate, not confirmed receiver:** `FUN_00C0CA70` may dispatch a `DisItemContext_UsePower` instance. Proving this requires recovering the class/vtable of receiver pointers stored at `entry+0x1E0`, or a read-only runtime trace that observes the receiver and its vptr.
- **Unknown:** the semantic meaning of the record key at `+0x1D4`, the source and meaning of the three arguments, which call path creates or registers these records, whether any caller corresponds to `CastBlink`, and whether the observed route is triggered by keyboard/gamepad Use.
- **Separate branch:** the Blink `+0x340` route and the `FUN_00CF34E0` / `0x011BD230` pointer sequence are not joined to this candidate. No ownership is assigned to `0x011C55A0` or `0x011BD230`.
- **Runtime limitation:** no runtime observation is included. Do not launch the game for this experiment; a future runtime check requires a user-started matching build and read-only inspection tools.
